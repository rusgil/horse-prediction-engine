New to racing?

Learn the basics from the sport's own authority

MyHorse.Tips is a predictions engine. These are clear, authoritative starting points for anyone new to the sport: the terminology, how track ratings work, the bet types, and how to make sense of a race.

Racing 101 — Racing Victoria ↗

An authoritative beginner's guide to Australian racing: racing terms and glossary, track ratings (Firm to Heavy), bet types, and how races work. Opens on racingvictoria.com.au.

Racing Explained — The Jockey Club ↗

A broader, global primer on the sport: a day at the races, reading a racecard, odds and bet types, flat vs jumps, and a full glossary. Opens on thejockeyclub.co.uk.
