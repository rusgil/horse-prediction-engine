About MyHorse.Tips

MyHorse.Tips is an independent AI-assisted analysis service for Australian thoroughbred racing. We rate every runner in every race and publish the selections our model backs, alongside free educational guides to the sport. We are not a bookmaker and we do not take bets.

MyHorse.Tips provides information and predictions for entertainment purposes only — nothing here is betting or financial advice. Gamble responsibly. If gambling is a problem for you, call Gambling Help on 1800 858 858 (Australia). 18+.
