How the Model Works
🧠
Numerous Predictive Signals
The model synthesises numerous independent signals per runner — spanning form, connections, breeding, race conditions, and market behaviour. Each factor is weighted by its historical predictive power and updated as new outcomes arrive.
📊
Market vs Model
Every pick is benchmarked against the current market price. The model calculates its own implied win probability and compares it to what the odds imply. When the gap is significant — the model rates the horse materially higher than the market — that difference is shown as the Edge %.
📈
Continuously Learning
FunkyIQ never stops thinking. Behind the scenes, our engine is constantly digesting outcomes, refining patterns, and sharpening its edge — so the picks you see today reflect everything we've learned yesterday.
The Tiers Explained
💎
Premium Pick
The rarest signal — where high model confidence lands on a longer-priced runner the model rates above the market. The intersection of a strong pick and a value overlay. Premium marks the model's highest-conviction value calls; because they sit at longer odds they carry more variance, so treat them as a signal to weigh, not a profit promise.
🔥
Hot Pick
The model's highest-conviction tier. Hot Picks are races where the model assigns ≥45% win probability to the top-ranked runner. Live win rate tracking is in progress — results update daily as genuine pre-race predictions accumulate.
⚡
High Confidence
Races where the model assigns 35–45% win probability. This tier captures a higher volume of picks while maintaining strong selection accuracy. Live win rate tracking is in progress.
✦
Strong Pick
Races where the model assigns 30–34% win probability to the top-ranked runner. The broadest confidence tier — represents races where the model has a clear top pick with meaningful separation from the rest of the field. Live win rate tracking is in progress.
