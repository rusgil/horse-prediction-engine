How the Model Works
🧠
36 Predictive Signals
The model synthesises 36 independent factors per runner: recent form, win rates by distance and track, surface suitability, barrier draw, days since last run, class change, trainer and jockey patterns, pedigree signals, gear changes, weight vs field, live market rank, and live odds movement in the hour before jump. Each factor is weighted by its historical predictive power.
📊
Market vs Model
Every pick is benchmarked against the current market price. The model calculates its own implied win probability and compares it to what the odds imply. When the gap is significant — the model rates the horse materially higher than the market — that difference is shown as the Edge %.
📈
Continuously Learning
FunkyIQ never stops thinking. Behind the scenes, our engine is constantly digesting outcomes, refining patterns, and sharpening its edge — so the picks you see today reflect everything we've learned yesterday.

The Tiers Explained
💎
Premium Pick
The rarest signal. A Premium Pick requires all three conditions to align simultaneously: the model rates the horse at ≥30% win probability, the market is offering $3.00 or better, and the model's probability is at least 5% higher than what the odds imply. This is the intersection of model confidence and market underpricing.
🔥
Hot Pick
The model's highest-conviction tier. Hot Picks are races where the model assigns ≥45% win probability to the top-ranked runner. Live win rate tracking is in progress — results update daily as genuine pre-race predictions accumulate.
⚡
High Confidence
Races where the model assigns 35–45% win probability. This tier captures a higher volume of picks while maintaining strong selection accuracy. Live win rate tracking is in progress.
✦
Strong Pick
The broadest tier — model confidence of ≥30%. Strong Picks represent races where the model has a clear top-ranked selection with meaningful separation from the rest of the field. Live win rate tracking is in progress.
